# Smart Expense & Budget Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement

Managing personal income and expenses manually can make it difficult to track spending, maintain budgets, and understand financial patterns.

The Smart Expense & Budget Tracker is designed to provide a centralized system for recording financial transactions, managing category-wise budgets, monitoring spending, and generating financial reports.

---

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on personal finance management through a web-based application.

The system allows users to:

- Record income and expense transactions.
- Organize transactions into categories.
- Create monthly budgets for different categories.
- Monitor budget usage and remaining amounts.
- Receive alerts when spending approaches or exceeds a budget.
- Analyze category-wise and monthly spending.
- Generate monthly financial reports.
- Store and retrieve financial data using MySQL.

The project is designed as a single-user personal finance management system.

---

## 3. Target Users

The primary target users are:

- Students who want to manage their monthly expenses.
- Individuals who want to track personal income and spending.
- Users who want to monitor category-wise budgets.
- Users who need simple financial summaries and reports.

---

## 4. High-Level Features

### Transaction Management
- Add income and expense transactions.
- View transaction history.
- Delete transactions.
- Validate transaction details.

### Category Management
- Organize transactions using categories.
- Retrieve available categories from the database.

### Budget Management
- Create monthly category-wise budgets.
- Track spending against budgets.
- Calculate remaining budget.
- Generate budget usage alerts.

### Analytics
- Display total income and expenses.
- Calculate financial balance.
- Analyze category-wise spending.
- Display monthly spending trends.

### Reports
- Generate monthly financial reports.
- Display income, expenses, and balance.
- Display transactions for the selected period.
